What time should you get up tomorrow?

It sounds like an easy question, but it isn't. It all depends on what you need to do before you leave: a shower, breakfast, a coffee, walking the dog… The only thing you really know is when you have to be out of the door.

Parakeet does the math for you. Set your exit time, pick the activities you'll do tomorrow morning, and Parakeet works out when to wake you up: not a minute earlier, not a minute later.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set the time you leave home.
• Tap the activities you'll do tomorrow: each one has a duration you can change.
• At the top you instantly see your wake-up time, the day, and how long you need to get ready.
• Tap "Set alarm" and the alarm is created in your phone's Clock app, without duplicates.
• At your exit time Parakeet can send you a notification ("Time to leave!"), with a text of your choice.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• One clear screen: the result is always in front of you, on a radio-clock style display.
• Your activities, your durations: add, edit and delete whenever you like (swipe to delete, with "Undo" if you slip).
• A short guided first run with a preview of your wake-up time.
• Light and dark theme, designed for tablets too.
• No account, no ads, no network connection: your activities stay on your device.

UPDATING FROM THE PREVIOUS VERSION
Your activities and exit time are kept automatically.

The alarm is created in the system Clock app. For the notification at exactly your exit time, Parakeet may ask you to allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders" access: if you'd rather not, the app still works, but the notification may arrive a few minutes late.

It's everything you need to get up early, but not a minute earlier. Try Parakeet today, wake up happier tomorrow.
