What time should you get up tomorrow?

It sounds like an easy question, but it isn't. It all depends on what you need to do before you leave: a shower, breakfast, a coffee, walking the dog… The only thing you really know is when you have to be out of the door.

Parakeet does the math for you. Set your exit time, pick the activities you'll do in the morning, and Parakeet works out when to wake you up: not a minute earlier, not a minute later. And the alarm rings right in the app.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set the time you leave home.
• Tap the activities you'll do: each one has a duration you can change.
• At the top you instantly see your wake-up time, the day, and how long you need to get ready, on a radio-clock style display.
• The alarm rings by itself, even over the lock screen, with the sound of your choice and gradually rising volume.
• At your exit time Parakeet can send you a notification ("Time to leave!"), with a text of your choice.

TWO WAYS TO USE IT
• Simple: your exit time and tomorrow's activities, one alarm at a time.
• Scheduled: create your routines (workdays, weekend, gym…) and assign them to weekdays. Parakeet re-arms the alarm by itself, every day. A different day this week? Change just that one, skipping it is one tap.
You choose at first start and can switch any time in Settings, without losing anything.

TURN IT OFF YOUR WAY
Pick how to stop the alarm: slide or long-press, solve a sum, shake the phone, or scan a QR code of your choice (the coffee pot in the kitchen, say). Snooze is optional.

HELPFUL REMINDERS (OPTIONAL)
• A silent notification before the alarm, so you can skip it if you're already up.
• An evening summary with tomorrow's alarm and a "Skip" button.

MORE PHONES, ONE PLAN (OPTIONAL)
Pick a folder (synced with Syncthing or your own cloud service, say) and Parakeet keeps your activities, routines and settings aligned across your devices. Parakeet connects to nothing: it only writes to the folder you choose. In Settings you can also save and restore a backup file.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Your activities, your durations: add, edit and delete whenever you like.
• A short guided first run with a preview of your wake-up time.
• Light and dark theme, designed for tablets too.
• No account, no ads, no Internet connection: your activities stay on your device (or in the folder you choose).

UPDATING FROM THE PREVIOUS VERSION
Your activities and exit time are kept automatically. If you prefer, you can keep using the system Clock app: the choice is in Settings.

PERMISSIONS
• Alarms & reminders, notifications and full-screen: needed so the alarm rings at the exact time and shows over the lock screen.
• Camera: only if you choose QR code dismissal. The image is processed on the phone and is never saved or sent.

It's everything you need to get up early, but not a minute earlier. Try Parakeet today, wake up happier tomorrow.
